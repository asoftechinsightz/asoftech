## UI principles

- Premium enterprise SaaS look with subtle gradients and restrained motion.
- Text-based brand mark until official logo assets exist.
- No invented client logos, testimonials, awards, certifications, case studies, or fake metrics.
- Conceptual product mockups are labelled as conceptual.
- CareerEdge360 never claims guaranteed jobs or placements.
